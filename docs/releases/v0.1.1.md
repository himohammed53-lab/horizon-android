# Horizon Android:

Unofficial Android fork of Horizon (Rust Minecraft 26.3), developed by Muhamed Al-zarqi (محمد آل زوركي) and Claude.

Based on the Android port of Horizon by Vasysik (https://github.com/Vasysik/horizon-android), which is based on Horizon by Ludovic Marie and the Horizon contributors (https://github.com/ludovic111/horizon, MIT license).

This is the first release of this fork. Mods are separate .hmod files and are not part of this download.

## Changes
- Launcher: the outer launcher look was redesigned, with larger lists and buttons, and some old built-in items and mods removed.
- Mod support: the app code was extended to support more mods, including a Waypoints mod, a Mods button in the pause menu and a Mod Menu-style list with details.
- Controls and layout: the on-screen touch layout now appears inside the settings screens and outside them, and can be turned on and off with a button.
- Zoom: the built-in zoom now goes up to 100x.
- Mouse sensitivity setting added.

## Not verified
Tested by the maintainer on one phone (Realme GT Neo 5 SE). Other devices and Android versions have not been verified.
