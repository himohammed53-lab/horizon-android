# Horizon Android 0.1.1

Unofficial Android fork of Horizon (Rust Minecraft 26.3), developed by **Muhamed Al-zarqi (محمد آل زوركي)** and **Claude**. It is based on Vasysik's Android port of Horizon (Ludovic Marie and the Horizon contributors, MIT).

This is the first release of this fork. Mods are separate `.hmod` files and are not part of this download.

## Launcher
- Dark theme, an Enderman-eyes background, purple glass buttons, larger Play button and tabs.
- Larger rows, fonts and buttons inside the Mods, Packs, Shaders, Files and Skins lists.
- Footer credits the developers; the GitHub button was removed and the repository link points to this fork.
- An ON/OFF switch, a shorter Play page, and no source-only rows.

## Touch controls and menus
- Touch buttons stay available in the inventory, crafting and settings screens.
- LMB/RMB click buttons for menus (hidden until BT is ON), a BT ON/OFF button, and a button that opens the Touch Controls setup.
- The attack button no longer turns the camera: hold attack with one thumb and look with the other.
- Fixed sneaking in the inventory; mouse sensitivity beside camera sensitivity, up to 600%.

## Zoom and graphics
- Zoom levels up to 100x; the in/out keys work without holding the zoom key.
- 60 FPS default cap on Android.
- Graphics settings keep the original Horizon look.

## Mods
- Falling Trees, Vein Miner and Gamma are now separate drop-in `.hmod` files instead of being built in; Zoom stays built in.
- A Mod Menu-style list with a details pane and mod icons, and a Mods button (M) in the pause menu.
- Mod API levels 8 to 11: `render.set-brightness`, `world.break-blocks`, `game.get-view`, list colours and text input, `game.get-world` and `ui.set-slider`.
- Mod screens: list scroll bars can be held and dragged by touch or mouse, a list scrolls with the right button held, mod keys can be picked with the system keyboard, and list colours use the correct channel order.
- A world recreated under the same folder name no longer inherits the old world's per-mod data.

## Not verified
Tested by the maintainer on one phone (Realme GT Neo 5 SE). Other devices and Android versions have not been verified, and neither has showing other players' skins in multiplayer for players who are not signed in to a Mojang account.
